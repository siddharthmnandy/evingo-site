    How we handle your waitlist detailsPlain-language notice · November 2026

    What we collect

    What you type into the waitlist form: your name, business name, business email, business type, city, phone number if you give one, your interest level, and your consent to be emailed. We also note which website the form was sent from.

    Why

    Only to contact you about Evingo: product updates, pilot opportunities and pre-orders. We don't sell, rent or trade your details, and we don't add you to anyone else's list.

    Where it's kept

    Submissions go to a Google Form and are stored in a Google Sheet in the Evingo founders' Google account, on Google's servers, which may be outside Canada. Only the Evingo team can open it.

    On your device

    Your browser keeps a scrambled (hashed) copy of the email you signed up with, so the form can tell you if you've already joined. There are no tracking cookies or analytics on this site. Fonts and code libraries load from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which see your IP address like any website you visit.

    Getting your details removed

    Reply to any email from Evingo and ask, and we'll delete your details from the list. You can also unsubscribe at any time.

    Waitlist only. Evingo collects no patient data here.

    Your business. Your hardware. Your data.

    Evingo uses a local-first design: the intelligence lives on hardware in your office, so more of your business information stays in your hands.

    On the unitDesigned to stay
    • Local processingCore AI processing is designed to happen directly on the business's hardware.
    • On-site dataCustomer information and appointment records can remain within the business's local environment.
    Leaves your office
    • The phone call itselfCalls reach you through your phone carrier, like any other call.
    • Calendar sync, if you turn it onConnecting an online calendar shares booking details with that service. It follows its own privacy practices.

    Greater control. Reduce dependence on third-party cloud AI providers and keep more control over business information.

    Built around privacy. A local-first approach gives businesses more ownership over their technology. Less leaves the office — we won't claim nothing ever does.

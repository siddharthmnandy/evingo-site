    Your patients' data stays on hardware you own.

    Evingo uses a local-first design: the intelligence lives on hardware in your office, so more of your business information stays in your hands.

    Designed to stay on the unit

    • Local processingCore AI processing is designed to happen directly on the business's hardware.
    • On-site dataCustomer information and appointment records can remain within the business's local environment.

    Leaves your office

    • The phone call itselfCalls reach you through your phone carrier, like any other call.
    • Calendar sync, if you turn it onConnecting an online calendar shares booking details with that service. It follows its own privacy practices.

    Greater control. Reduce dependence on third-party cloud AI providers and keep more control over business information.

    Built around privacy. A local-first approach gives businesses more ownership over their technology. Less leaves the office — we won't claim nothing ever does.

    Your clinic stays the trustee.

    Under The Health Information Protection Act (HIPA), the clinic is the trustee of patient information. A company that stores or processes that information for you is an information management service provider, and HIPA requires a written agreement with it. Since August 1, 2023, those agreements must cover the terms set out in the updated regulations.

    Evingo's local-first design keeps call records on the unit in your office, so less information leaves the clinic in the first place. We plan to provide a HIPA-ready service agreement to every clinic before a pilot starts.
